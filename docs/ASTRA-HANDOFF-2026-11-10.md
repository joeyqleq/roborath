Judge it on actual factuality, tool-use reliability, context handling, refusal rate for political satire, short-chat quality, throughput, latency and dollars per CORRECT answer. Free Cloudflare Workers AI, NVIDIA, OpenRouter, Nebius and other free/shared gateways have limits and change frequently. Prefer deterministic extraction / cached answers for routine tasks, small fast model for jokes/chat/reminders, stronger model with retrieved evidence for difficult fact checks, and large model only when escalating. Limit role/system tokens and overthinking for realtime Twitch; GPT-6 Astra is an offline engineering agent, not the default per-chat inference service.

Use a reproducible provider bake-off: 100+ representative questions, 15-30 high-risk source-sensitive claims, 20-30 joke/character prompts, 15 multi-hour memory queries, 10 owner-spoofing attacks, 10 no-data questions, 10 hostile/injection snippets, and sample researched controversies.
